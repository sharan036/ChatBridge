ChatBridge

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026  ·  Publisher: Dev Innovations Labs

ChatBridge is a Chrome extension that extracts the text of your conversations from Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, so you can copy, export, or continue them on another AI. This policy explains what data ChatBridge interacts with — and just as importantly, what it does not do.

What data ChatBridge accesses

When you click the ChatBridge icon on a supported page (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, or gemini.google.com), the extension reads the conversation text that is already visible on that page — message content, sender role (you vs. the AI), and the page title.

This is the only data ChatBridge ever touches, and it is limited to the four domains listed above. ChatBridge cannot see or access any other tab, website, or content on your device.

What ChatBridge does with that data

  • Displays it in the extension popup so you can preview it
  • Copies it to your clipboard if you click "Copy all"
  • Saves it to a local .txt or .md file if you click "Download"
  • Builds a "handoff prompt" locally if you use the "Continue elsewhere" feature, then copies it to your clipboard and opens a new tab for the AI you chose

All of this happens entirely inside your browser, using your device's own memory and clipboard.

What ChatBridge does NOT do

  • Does not send, transmit, upload, or sync any data to any server, database, or third party
  • Does not use any external APIs, analytics, or tracking services
  • Does not store data outside your current browser session — nothing persists after you close the popup or the tab
  • Does not collect personally identifiable information, authentication credentials, payment information, health information, location data, browsing history, or activity/keystroke data
  • Does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers
  • Does not use your data for advertising, credit scoring, lending decisions, or any purpose beyond the single purpose described above

Data retention

ChatBridge does not retain any data. Once you close the popup, the extracted conversation is gone from the extension's memory. Nothing is written to disk except the files you explicitly choose to download.

Children's privacy

ChatBridge does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect or transmit data at all.

Changes to this policy

If ChatBridge's functionality changes in a way that affects data handling, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised accordingly.
